**Perplexity Pro** is the specialist seat for live-web research with citations: **$20/month**, or **$17/month** when billed annually. **ChatGPT Plus** is **$20/month** (monthly only) and fits better when Deep Research feeds into drafting, custom GPTs, and a general workbench. Same monthly sticker. Pick Perplexity when the answer must cite the open web. Pick ChatGPT when research sits next to writing and tools in one chat.

Prices checked **16 Sep 2026**. Model names on marketing pages move often. Use the in-product model picker as source of truth. Search ranks and volumes are unknown.

No affiliate tracking on either consumer plan. Official URLs only.

## What is each tool actually good at for research?

### Perplexity Pro

Perplexity is built as an answer engine: ask a question, get a cited reply from the live web, then dig. Hub pricing lists Deep research, access to top AI models, model selection, file/create workflows, and higher usage than Free.

The help article adds: more citations per answer, extended Research, higher file/photo upload limits, image generation, limited video generation, and Pro Search volume for multi-step work. You can switch models and search modes in-session.

That shape fits competitive scans, "what did X announce this week," pricing checks, and any brief where you want sources you can open. Citations are the product.

### ChatGPT Plus

OpenAI Help states Plus is **$20/month**, billed monthly, with no annual option for Plus. Relative to Free you get higher model limits, priority during busy periods, and expanded access to voice, image generation, file uploads, and **Deep Research tools where available**.

The consumer pricing matrix also lists advanced reasoning models, projects, scheduled tasks, custom GPTs, expanded Codex, and ChatGPT Work across desktop/web/mobile. Exact message caps are not a fixed public number. They flex with load.

That shape fits research that turns into a draft, a table, a GPT, or a coding follow-up without leaving the chat. Deep Research is strong when you want a long synthesized report. Citation behavior is different from Perplexity's default inline web footnotes. Verify claims the same way you would with any model.

## Which should you pick for live-web research with sources?

**Perplexity Pro** when the job is "search the open web and show me where that came from." Inline citations, Pro Search, and Deep research on the Perplexity side are aimed at that loop.

**ChatGPT Plus** when the job is "research, then write, plan, or build in the same seat." Deep Research plus projects and GPTs is the draw. You will still open primary sources yourself for anything money- or legal-shaped.

If you only need occasional cited lookups, stay on **Perplexity Free** first. If you only need light ChatGPT research, **Go at $8/mo** or Free may be enough before Plus.

MorningStacks has not run a first-person A/B on answer quality between these two for this piece. The pick above is job-fit from official feature lists and pricing, not a lab score.

## When does Max or ChatGPT Pro make sense?

**Perplexity Max ($200/month)** if you burn Pro caps on Computer, expert research, or frontier-model volume. The hub page pitches highest usage limits, 10,000 monthly credits, and priority features. Most solo operators will not need this on day one.

**ChatGPT Pro** if Plus Deep Research and reasoning caps keep biting. OpenAI Help notes the **$200** Pro tier (20×) has **new sign-ups and upgrades paused as of 10 Sep 2026**; existing $200 subs and the **$100** (5×) tier are called out as unaffected in that note. Confirm what checkout shows you.

Paying either Max or ChatGPT Pro "for research" before you know your weekly query volume is vanity. Hit a real wall first.

## Can you run both without wasting money?

Yes. A common solo stack is Perplexity Pro for source-heavy scans and ChatGPT Plus for drafting and tool work. That is **$40/month** if both are monthly, or closer to **$37/month** if Perplexity is annual ($17) plus Plus ($20).

If you can only fund one seat, buy the one that matches the bottleneck you hit every week:

- Citations and live web → Perplexity Pro
- Drafts, GPTs, Deep Research reports, Codex → ChatGPT Plus

Do not buy Max and ChatGPT Pro together unless you have usage logs that justify both.

## Recommendation

Default to **Perplexity Pro** for research-shaped work that needs the open web and citations. Default to **ChatGPT Plus** when research is one step inside a broader writing and tooling loop.

Same **$20/month** sticker for Pro monthly vs Plus. Annual billing favors Perplexity ($17/mo equivalent on the Pro page we read). Plus has no annual path on OpenAI Help.

Stay on free tiers until the caps annoy you. Then pay one seat, not both, until the second seat earns its keep.
