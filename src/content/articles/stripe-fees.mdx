Stripe fees start at **2.9% plus 30 cents** for a US card payment. That headline rate is accurate, and it's also the least interesting part of your Stripe bill.

The real number depends on where your customer's card was issued, whether you sell subscriptions, how often you get disputes, and how small your average sale is. We pulled every line below from Stripe's US pricing page on **8 October 2026** and worked through what it means on real prices.

## What does Stripe charge per transaction in the US?

These are the standard, pay-as-you-go rates. There's no setup fee, monthly fee, or closure fee on standard pricing.

<div class="table-wrap">

| Payment method | Fee per successful payment |
| --- | --- |
| Domestic cards and wallets | 2.9% + 30¢ |
| Manually entered card | +0.5% |
| International card | +1.5% |
| Currency conversion needed | +1% |
| ACH Direct Debit | 0.8%, capped at $5.00 |
| Instant bank payments (Link) | 2.6% + 30¢ |
| Pix and UPI (Link) | 1.8% + 30¢ |
| Klarna (buy now, pay later) | 5.99% + 30¢ |
| Stablecoins (Payments) | 1.5% of the USD amount |
| In-person cards (Terminal) | 2.7% + 5¢ |

</div>

Source: [Stripe pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing). The surcharges stack. A foreign card that also needs currency conversion costs 2.9% + 1.5% + 1%, so 5.4% plus 30 cents.

Stablecoin payments through Link are 0.8% on a promotional rate that runs through 1 January 2027, then 0.2% more after that, per the same page.

## Which Stripe fees catch people out?

### International cards and currency conversion

If you sell software worldwide, a big share of your customers may pay with foreign cards. Each of those costs an extra 1.5%, and another 1% if Stripe has to convert currency.

Adaptive Pricing, which shows prices in the buyer's local currency, is included on standard pricing. Stripe says the customer sees a conversion fee starting at 2%, so the cost moves to your buyer.

### Disputes

A dispute (chargeback) costs **$15** the moment you receive it. If you fight it yourself, there's a **$15 countered fee**, which comes back if you win. Smart Disputes, where Stripe assembles the evidence, costs **30% of the disputed amount** on wins only.

So a lost $40 dispute costs you the $40, the original processing fee, and $15 to $30 on top.

### Refunds

Stripe doesn't charge to issue a refund on most methods. It also keeps the original processing fee. Refund a $100 card payment and you're out $3.20. Bank-transfer refunds can carry their own fee.

### Instant payouts

Standard payouts are free. If you want your money in minutes, Instant Payouts cost **1.5%** of the amount, with a 50 cent minimum.

### In-person payments

If you also sell face to face with Stripe Terminal, the US rate is **2.7% plus 5 cents** for domestic cards, plus 1.5% for international cards. Tap to Pay on a compatible iPhone or Android phone adds $0.10 per authorization. Optional point-to-point encryption adds $0.05 per authorization.

A $40 sale in person on a domestic card costs $1.13. The same sale online costs $1.46.

## What do Billing, Invoicing, and Tax add?

Subscriptions and tax are where a Stripe bill grows beyond 2.9%. These are all on top of the payment fee.

<div class="table-wrap">

| Product | Standard price |
| --- | --- |
| Billing (pay as you go) | 0.7% of Billing volume |
| Billing (pay monthly) | From $620/month, 1-year contract |
| Invoicing Starter | 0.4% per paid invoice |
| Post-payment invoices (Checkout, Payment Links) | 0.4%, capped at $2.00 per invoice |
| Tax Basic | 0.5% per transaction where you're registered |
| Tax Complete | From $90/month, 1-year contract |
| Radar (fraud) | Radar Lite included; paid plans from $10/month, or from $0.05 per screened transaction |
| Custom domain for Checkout or portal | $10/month |
| Identity verification | $1.50 per verification |

</div>

Sources: [Stripe pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing), [Billing pricing](https://stripe.com/billing/pricing), and [Tax pricing](https://stripe.com/tax/pricing).

Here's a $20/month subscription on a domestic card with Billing and Tax Basic: 2.9% + 30¢ is $0.88, Billing is $0.14, Tax is $0.10. That's **$1.12, or 5.6%**, every month. Bill annually at $200 and the same stack costs $8.50 a year (4.3%), against $13.44 for 12 monthly charges, because you only pay the 30 cents once.

## What is Stripe Managed Payments?

Managed Payments makes Stripe your **merchant of record**. Stripe handles indirect tax (sales tax, VAT, and GST) and remittance in more than 75 countries, plus fraud, disputes, and customer support for those sales.

It costs **3.5% per transaction on top of the normal payment fees**. On a $20 domestic sale that's $0.88 plus $0.70.

That's a lot next to Tax Basic at 0.5%. What you're paying for is not having to register for and file VAT and sales tax yourself. For a solo founder selling digital products to buyers in dozens of countries, that trade can make sense. For a US-only business, it rarely does.

## How do you lower your Stripe fees?

Practical moves, using only Stripe's published rates:

1. **Push annual plans.** You pay the 30 cent fee once a year instead of 12 times. On a $20 plan that saves $3.30 a year per customer in fixed fees alone.
2. **Offer ACH for large invoices.** At a $5 cap, it's the cheapest US option by far above $625.
3. **Avoid tiny charges.** Bundling three $5 add-ons into one $15 charge saves 60 cents.
4. **Don't key cards by hand.** Manual entry adds 0.5%.
5. **Fight disputes you can win.** The $15 countered fee comes back if you win.
6. **Ask about custom pricing.** Stripe offers volume discounts and custom packages for large or unusual businesses. It doesn't publish the threshold.
