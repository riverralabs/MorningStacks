**Systeme.io** is the cheaper default for a solo operator: **Free at $0** (2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, 1 course) and **Startup at $17/month**. **ClickFunnels Launch** starts at **$97/month** with a 14-day trial and no free plan. Start on Systeme Free unless you already need ClickFunnels workspaces, onboarding culture, or API access (API starts at **Scale, $197/month**).

Prices checked **16 Sep 2026** on official pricing pages. Search ranks and volumes are unknown.

MorningStacks has **no live affiliate** for either vendor. Links go to official pricing and help pages only.

## What is the live price gap in September 2026?

### Systeme.io

Official [systeme.io/pricing](https://systeme.io/pricing). JSON-LD on the page (16 Sep 2026) lists monthly USD offers. FAQ on that page (stamp: April 2026) matches the same stickers.

| Plan | Official monthly (16 Sep 2026) | Contacts | Funnels / courses (FAQ) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 2,000 | 3 funnels, 1 course |
| Startup | $17/month | 5,000 | Unlimited funnels, 5 courses |
| Webinar | $47/month | 10,000 | Automated webinars added |
| Unlimited | $97/month | Unlimited | Sub-accounts + free migration (FAQ) |

All plans, per that FAQ: unlimited email sends, 0% platform transaction fees (Stripe or PayPal). Free also lists 1 blog, 1 affiliate program, and 1 custom domain.

**Annual dollars:** an annual toggle exists on the pricing UI. We did **not** confirm yearly totals in the `/pricing` JSON on this fetch. Do not paste third-party recaps that still say Startup is $27/month. Official FAQ and JSON win at **$17/month**.

### ClickFunnels

Unlimited funnels and visitors are marked **Limited Time** on every tier. Expiry of that promo was not on the page. ClickFunnels FAQ schema describes a 14-day trial plus a 30-day money-back window (44 days combined, as their FAQ states). No platform cut of sales, per FAQ.

A $99 partner trial appears in third-party recaps. **It was not on the official pricing HTML we saved.** Omit it.

Systeme.io's pricing FAQ claims a comparable ClickFunnels + Mailchimp + Teachable + Tapfiliate stack at $245/month. That stack math is a vendor claim. We are not repeating it as fact.

ClickFunnels Launch matches Systeme Unlimited's monthly sticker ($97), with a free Systeme tier underneath that ClickFunnels does not offer.

If you need webhooks and a full API as a solo builder, ClickFunnels does not give those on Launch. They start at Scale ($197/month). People compare $97 to $17 and miss that the API sits one tier up.

## When is Systeme.io's free plan enough for a first offer?

When all of this is true:

- You have fewer than 2,000 contacts.
- You can live with 3 funnels and 1 course.
- Stripe or PayPal is enough (0% platform fee claimed).
- You do not need ClickFunnels-style workspaces, group onboarding, or an API.

The honest Free downside is design depth and how long automations take to learn. If your offer is a simple checkout plus a sequence, Free is enough. If you sell with a dozen variants and custom code, you are already shopping ClickFunnels Scale or a specialist stack, not Systeme Free.

## When does ClickFunnels Launch still win for a solo founder?

Launch still wins when you need the ClickFunnels workspace model, their editor, community/course counts as packaged on that page, or you already know their onboarding calls matter to you. It also wins if your team is two people on day one. Systeme Free is not a team product in the way Launch's 2 team members / 1 workspace is.

It loses on price. $97/month versus $0 or $17 is not close. It also loses on "unlimited email sends" as Systeme FAQ states those on every plan, while Launch caps emails at 50K/month.

Scale wins over Launch when you need API/webhooks, live chat support, or 75K contacts. That is a different buyer than "solo, first offer."

Dominate at $5,997/year is not a solo-operator plan. We list it so the table is complete.

## What happens if you blow Systeme.io contact limits?

Official help: [What happens when my account gets restricted](https://help.systeme.io/article/1086-what-happens-when-my-account-gets-restricted) (fetched earlier in this ship cycle; re-open on Promote if the stamp moves).

Contact caps: Freemium 2,000; Startup 5,000; Webinar 10,000; Unlimited unlimited.

Four-day pre-restriction with three warning emails, per that help article.

- **Freemium restriction** stops opt-ins, payments, new contacts, scheduled newsletters, and new automation triggers.
- **Paid plans** auto-upgrade to the next tier if limits are exceeded.

That auto-upgrade is the part people miss. Startup is cheap until a viral week promotes you to Webinar at $47, then Unlimited at $97. Budget for the cap, not only for the sticker you clicked.

ClickFunnels Launch starts at 10K contacts. If list size is the only constraint and you refuse auto-upgrades, Launch has more room than Systeme Startup. You are then paying $97 for that room. Systeme Unlimited at the same $97 removes the contact ceiling.

## Can you switch or cancel without losing the list?

Export the list before you switch. That is the whole risk.

Systeme Unlimited FAQ claims free migration. We did not run a migration. Treat that as a vendor offer to book, not as a completed project.

ClickFunnels: you can change plans during or after the trial (FAQ). 30-day money-back after the 14-day trial, per their FAQ schema. Hosting is included. Custom domains are supported; they will give a `myclickfunnels.com` subdomain if you have none.

Neither vendor should be your only copy of emails and customers. Keep CSV exports on a schedule.

White-label Systeme.io: FAQ says contact the team. No public price. Do not invent one.

## Do affiliate programs change the pick?

You may never promote either tool. If you do, use vendor numbers, not rumor.

**Systeme.io** publishes a public affiliate program (60% lifetime recurring on vendor pages; help articles cover cookie and payout rules). Official tracking uses `?sa=ID`. Marketing and help have disagreed on holding periods in older scrapes. Re-read both before you rely on a click.

**ClickFunnels** publishes a public affiliate program (vendor page: "Earn 30% Monthly Recurring Commission"). Application form. We did not verify a public query-string tracking pattern.

MorningStacks commission reality today: **$0**, because we have not applied. Do not treat any MorningStacks URL as a tracking link for these vendors.

## Recommendation

Start on **Systeme.io Free**. Pay **Startup at $17/month** when you need more than 3 funnels or 1 course, or when you are approaching 2,000 contacts. Pay **Unlimited at $97/month** when contacts or sub-accounts force it, which is also the first time Systeme matches Launch's monthly sticker.

Choose **ClickFunnels Launch at $97/month** only if you already know you need that workspace, editor, and onboarding culture, and you accept email caps and no API until Scale. Choose **Scale at $197/month** if the API is the product.

Do not pay Dominate. Do not pay Launch before checkout number one "to look serious."
