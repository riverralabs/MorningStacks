**Linear** and **Jira Cloud** both track issues, projects, and cycles for a product team. For a small SaaS crew (say 3-15 people), the fork is opinionated speed versus Atlassian depth and how Free caps bite.

Prices below come from [linear.app/pricing](https://linear.app/pricing) and [atlassian.com/software/jira/pricing](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira/pricing), checked **16 Sep 2026**. Search ranks and volumes unknown. MorningStacks has not migrated a live backlog between the two for a timed diary.

## What is Linear vs what is Jira for a small SaaS?

**Linear** is a cloud issue tracker with projects, cycles, initiatives, triage, and a heavy keyboard culture. Free allows unlimited members but caps **teams** and **issues**. Paid plans are per seat. Business adds guests, private teams, Asks, and more AI-shaped features on the pricing grid.

**Jira Cloud** is Atlassian's project tool with boards, backlogs, timelines, automation steps, and a long marketplace. Free covers **10** users. Standard and Premium are per user per month on the public cards. Enterprise is annual and sales-shaped. Confluence, Bitbucket, and Guard sit next door if you buy the wider suite.

Same category. Linear starts narrower and faster. Jira starts broader and more configurable.

## How do workflows feel for a 5-10 person SaaS?

**Linear** defaults to issues → projects → cycles. Triage and keyboard shortcuts keep standup noise low. The Free **250**-issue cap is the first wall for a busy product org. Hitting it means Basic, not "just archive harder" forever.

**Jira** defaults to projects with board types (Scrum/Kanban), workflows you can bend, and automation. Free's **10**-user cap is the first wall. Storage at **2 GB** can sting if you attach a lot of design dumps. Standard's **250 GB** and user-scaled automation usually fix that for a growing SaaS.

If your "small SaaS team" is mostly engineers who live in GitHub/GitLab PRs, Linear's issue sync and code-adjacent features on higher plans may feel closer to the desk. If PM, support, and eng all share one Atlassian login story with Confluence, Jira is the shorter political path.

## What about AI, asks, and intake?

Linear's pricing grid lists Agent platform / Linear Agent on Free, plus Triage Intelligence, Loops, and Code Intelligence on Business. Treat those names as plan gates on the live page; we didn't meter credit burn for coding sessions.

Jira's pricing table ties Rovo credits to paid plans (Standard **25**/user/mo, Premium **70**, Enterprise **150** on the page we fetched). Automation step budgets differ by plan (Free **150**/subscription; Standard **400**/user; Premium **750**/user).

Customer intake: Linear Asks (Business+) vs Jira forms and marketplace apps. For a tiny team, Slack + issues often wins before either intake product matters.

## Which tracker fits which small SaaS?

**Lean Linear when:**

- You want Free with unlimited members while issues stay under **250** and teams under **2**.
- The team will pay Basic (**$10**/user/mo yearly) for speed and a tight UI.
- You're fine without Confluence-shaped wiki adjacency on day one.

**Lean Jira when:**

- You're ≤**10** users and Free's board/backlog set is enough.
- You already buy Atlassian, or leadership expects Jira on the security questionnaire.
- You need Standard's storage, permissions depth, or a path to Premium roadmaps and SLA.

**Awkward on Linear Free:** multi-team SaaS with design + eng + success each wanting a team space past **2**, or a backlog that will cross **250** issues in a quarter.

**Awkward on Jira Free:** seat **11**, or heavy attachments chewing **2 GB**, or automation past **150** steps/mo for the whole site.

## How should you decide in one afternoon?

1. Count people who need a login this quarter. ≤10 → Jira Free is in play. Unlimited members on Linear Free still hits issue/team caps.
2. Count open + done issues you refuse to delete. Past ~200 active history → budget Linear Basic.
3. Open [linear.app/pricing](https://linear.app/pricing) and [Jira pricing](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira/pricing). Run Atlassian's calculator for your seat count.
4. Import a sample project both ways (both document import/export). Click through one sprint ritual.
5. Pick the tool your team will update daily. An empty Premium Jira loses to a lived-in Linear Basic.
