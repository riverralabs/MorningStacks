Zapier bills per successful **task** (Professional from **$19.99/mo** annual for 750 tasks). Make bills in **credits** (Free **1,000**/mo; Core **$12/mo** for **10k** credits on the annual view). n8n Cloud bills per **workflow execution** (Starter **€20/mo** annual with **2.5K** runs; Pro **€50/mo** annual with **10K** runs) and offers free self-host. Pick Zapier for catalog, Make for visual cost control, n8n for code or execution billing.

Prices checked **16 Sep 2026** against official pages. Currency mix is real: Zapier and Make showed USD; n8n showed EUR. Search ranks and volumes are unknown (Treg unavailable).

## How does each platform bill: task, credit, or execution?

This is the whole fight. Same workflow can cost different amounts on each tool because the meter is different.

**Zapier** counts a **task** when a step succeeds. Failed actions don't count. Triggers and several built-ins (Filter, Paths, Formatter, Delay, and friends) sit outside the task tally on the pricing docs we fetched. Multi-step Zaps eat tasks fast if every branch fires.

**Make** counts **credits**. As of late August 2025, Make renamed the old "operations" unit to credits; help docs still treat most module actions as one credit each. Routers and error handlers are called out as free of credit cost on the pricing FAQ. AI modules can burn more than one credit depending on tokens and connection type ([Make Credits help](https://help.make.com/credits), updated around 15 Sep 2026).

**n8n** counts a **workflow execution**: one full run from start to finish, no matter how many nodes sit inside. That is the pitch on [n8n pricing](https://n8n.io/pricing/): pay for the run, not every step.

If your automation is a long chain with lots of cheap transforms, n8n's meter often looks kinder on paper. If you fire many tiny one-step syncs all day, Zapier's task model can still be fine at low volume.

### Zapier detail

[Zapier Pricing](https://zapier.com/pricing) lists Free, Professional, Team, and Enterprise. Free is two-step Zaps and 100 tasks/mo. Professional adds multi-step Zaps, premium apps, and webhooks. ## Which tool fits a non-technical solo operator?

**Zapier** if you want the biggest connector list and the least time fighting the editor. Paths and filters are there, support is mature, and the Free tier is honest about being a trial for real multi-step work.

**Make** if you're willing to learn a visual scenario canvas and you care about credit efficiency on branching flows. Routers without credit cost help. The Free 1,000 credits are enough to learn; production usually means Core.

**n8n** if you're fine with a steeper editor, optional code nodes, and thinking in whole-run economics. Cloud Starter at €20 annual is the cheap on-ramp. Self-host if you're already comfortable with Docker.

MorningStacks default for a non-technical founder shipping newsletter → CRM → Slack glue: start on **Make Free or Core**, or **Zapier Free → Pro 750**, unless you already live in Git and containers.

## When does self-hosted n8n beat cloud Zapier or Make?

When you have:

1. Steady technical ops capacity (updates, backups, secrets, uptime).
2. Workflows that would explode per-step or per-credit bills (fat ETL, AI agent loops with many nodes).
3. Data residency or "keep it on our VPS" requirements.

Community Edition is free software. Your cost is infrastructure and your time. Business/Enterprise features on self-host still involve n8n's license story; read their current docs before you assume every Cloud feature comes with Community.

If you just want "form submit → Sheet → Slack" five times a day, self-hosting is usually overkill.

## How should AI steps change the cost math?

AI nodes change every vendor's meter.

On **Zapier**, AI by Zapier and related steps can consume more than one task depending on model tier and tool calls. The rates live on Zapier's rates page and move. Budget a cushion above your "normal" task count.

On **Make**, built-in AI provider usage can burn credits from tokens, not just a flat 1:1 operation. Bring-your-own-key paths still spend Make credits on the module run while you pay the model vendor separately ([credits help](https://help.make.com/credits)).

On **n8n**, the execution meter still counts one run, but Cloud plans include monthly **AI credits** for Assistant-style features (Starter lists 2,300/mo on the pricing page; Pro lists up to 13,700 depending on plan size). Those credits refresh monthly and don't roll over, per FAQ copy.

Rule of thumb we use: prototype AI steps early, watch one week's real usage, then pick the plan. Guessing AI burn from a marketing table is how people rage-quit month two.
