beehiiv is a newsletter platform built around publish, grow, and monetize in one product.

We are writing this for US and Tier 1 solo operators who need a newsletter stack without inventing first-person "we migrated" claims.

## What is beehiiv, and who is it for?

beehiiv sells newsletters, websites, podcasts, and community tools as one platform. The pitch is publish, grow, and earn without bolting together a separate site, ESP, and ad sales stack.

It fits solo operators and small media brands who:

- Want unlimited email sends without a per-send meter
- Care about monetization (paid subscriptions, ads, digital products) inside the same product
- Prefer a free runway up to 2,500 subscribers before paying

It is a weaker fit if you only need a simple ESP, if SMS is a must-have (beehiiv does not send SMS natively), or if you need enterprise deliverability and SSO on day one (Enterprise).

## How does beehiiv work?

1. Create a publication and import or grow a list.
2. Write and design in the newsletter builder.
3. Publish a matching no-code website if you want a public home for the brand.
4. Grow with recommendations, referrals, and analytics on paid tiers.
5. Monetize with paid subscriptions (beehiiv states a 0% take rate on paid subscription revenue, minus Stripe fees), the ad network on paid plans, and digital products.

Official FAQ ([beehiiv pricing](https://www.beehiiv.com/pricing), 10 September 2026): beehiiv takes 0% of paid subscription revenue. Stripe's fee is quoted as 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction. beehiiv contrasts that with Substack's 10% take. Treat that Substack figure as beehiiv's comparison claim, not an independent audit.

## Who should pay for Scale or Max?

**Stay on Launch** under 2,500 without ads or paid tools, and with branding OK.

**Move to Scale** for monetization features; confirm the calculator price first.

**Move to Max** for white-label, more than three publications, unlimited seats, dynamic content, or priority support.

**Talk to Enterprise** near or past 100k, or for SSO and dedicated IPs.

## How to start

1. Open [beehiiv pricing](https://www.beehiiv.com/pricing).
2. Start on Launch, or begin a Scale/Max trial if offered.
3. Import your list; confirm the active count.
4. Re-check pricing the day you pay; use the on-page calculator.
