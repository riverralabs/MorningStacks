**ChatGPT Plus** is **$20/mo** (monthly only). **Claude Pro** is **$20/mo** monthly or **$17/mo** annual (**$200** up front) and includes **Claude Code**. **Google AI Pro** is **$19.99/mo** on the US [gemini.google/subscriptions](https://gemini.google/subscriptions/) page, with Gemini, **5 TB** storage, and Gemini in Gmail and Docs. Same ballpark sticker. Pick ChatGPT for broad tooling and Codex, Claude for Claude Code, Google AI Pro if you live in Workspace.

Prices checked **16 Sep 2026**. Model names on these pages move every few weeks. Use the in-product model picker as source of truth for what your account can call today. Search ranks and volumes are unknown.

No affiliate tracking on any of these consumer plans. Links go to official pricing and help pages only.

## What does each Pro-tier plan actually include?

### ChatGPT Plus

OpenAI Help states Plus is **$20/month**, billed monthly, with no annual option for Plus. Relative to Free, you get higher model limits, priority during busy periods, and expanded access to tools like voice, image generation, file uploads, and Deep Research where available.

The consumer pricing matrix (chatgpt.com / OpenAI pricing pages) also lists Plus with advanced reasoning models, expanded Codex usage, ChatGPT Work on desktop/web/mobile, projects, scheduled tasks, and custom GPTs. Exact message caps aren't a fixed public number. They flex with load.

Below Plus sits **ChatGPT Go** at **$8/mo** in the US ([Go launch post](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-go/)): more messages and uploads than Free, without the full Plus reasoning stack. Above Plus sits **ChatGPT Pro** (see Max/Pro/Ultra section below).

API usage is separate. A Plus seat does not include API credits.

### Claude Pro

[Claude Pricing](https://claude.com/pricing) (same content mirrored on anthropic.com/pricing in our fetch): Pro is **$17/mo** with annual discount (**$200** billed up front) or **$20** if billed monthly.

Pro adds more usage than Free, **Claude Code**, Claude Cowork, Claude Design, Claude Science, Research, unlimited projects, and Claude for Microsoft 365. Free explicitly does **not** include Claude Code on the comparison grid we fetched.

Usage shares one pool across web, desktop, mobile, and Claude Code, with rolling limits (Anthropic describes five-hour windows plus weekly caps on paid plans). There is no honest fixed "messages per day" number on the marketing page.

### Google AI Pro

On the US [Gemini subscriptions](https://gemini.google/subscriptions/) page (curl, 16 Sep 2026): **Google AI Pro** lists at **$19.99/month**.

Marketing copy for Pro includes higher Gemini usage than Free (4x vs Free on that page), Google Flow credits, Gemini in Gmail/Docs and related apps, Jules and Google Antigravity at entry limits, Gemini Notebook with higher limits, and **5 TB** of Google storage across Gmail, Drive, and Photos. Bundles like YouTube Premium Lite and Google Home Premium Standard appear on the Google One AI plans page for Pro in eligible countries.

Help text also mentions **5 TB or 10 TB** storage variants for Google AI Pro. Confirm which SKU checkout shows you before you assume the $19.99 map.

Under Pro sits **Google AI Plus** at **$4.99/month** on that same US subscriptions fetch (400 GB storage on the Google One AI plans feature table). Above Pro sits Ultra.

## Which plan is better for coding work?

**Claude Pro** if you want a first-party coding agent (**Claude Code**) included in the chat subscription. That alone is why a lot of builders park here. Pair it with whatever editor you already use.

**ChatGPT Plus** if Codex and ChatGPT Work matter more to your loop than Claude Code. Plus is the practical coding seat for most people; ChatGPT Pro is the usage hammer when Plus caps bite.

**Google AI Pro** if your coding path is Jules, Antigravity, AI Studio, or Android Studio agent help inside Google's stack. Limits are "expanded" vs Free on the marketing pages. They are not unlimited.

None of these replace a serious IDE subscription on their own. For Cursor cutoff and related editor context, see [OpenAI Cursor cutoff notes](/ai-tools/openai-cursor-cutoff-november-2026/). For Claude model family context, see [Claude Fable 5.1 explained](/ai-tools/claude-fable-5-1-explained/).

## Which plan is better for research and writing?

**ChatGPT Plus** for general research with Deep Research tools, browsing, and a huge ecosystem of GPTs and connectors. It's the default "ask anything, draft anything" seat for many operators.

**Claude Pro** for long-context writing, project-organized work, and Research on Anthropic's models. If your drafts already sound better in Claude, pay for Pro and stop arguing with yourself.

**Google AI Pro** when the source material already sits in Drive, Gmail, Docs, or Sheets. Deep Research in Gemini plus Gemini-in-Docs is the product. You're buying workflow fit, not a religion.

For pure "search the live web and cite it," also weigh specialist research tools in a separate post. This piece sticks to the three Pro-class consumer seats.

## When should you pay for Max / Pro / Ultra instead?

Climb only when a lower seat blocks real work for days, not because a launch blog scared you.

**ChatGPT Pro:** Help docs describe **$100** as 5× Plus usage and **$200** as 20×. As of **10 Sep 2026**, OpenAI is **pausing new sign-ups and upgrades to Pro $200**. Existing $200 subs continue; new buyers can still move to **$100**. Re-read the Help note before you plan a $200 purchase.

**Claude Max:** Pricing cards show Max **from $100/mo** with a choice of **5× or 20×** usage vs Pro. We verified the **$100** "from" price for the 5× monthly card. A separate printed dollar for Max 20× did not show as its own sticker in the 16 Sep 2026 scrape (the **$200** figures on that page map to Claude Pro annual prepaid in the text we checked). Confirm 20× checkout before you budget it.

**Google AI Ultra:** Live US subscriptions page lists **$99.99/mo** (5× vs Pro) and **$199.99/mo** (20×), with storage starting at 20 TB and higher Gemini / Flow / Antigravity limits. Buy Ultra when Pro rate-limits are a weekly problem or you need the Ultra-only experiments Google gates (Project Genie is called out as Ultra-class on their pages).

Paying two Pro seats (for example Claude Pro + ChatGPT Plus) can be rational. Paying Max + ChatGPT Pro + Ultra on week one usually isn't.

## What’s the default pick for a solo operator?

**Live in Gmail, Docs, Drive all day:** Google AI Pro at **$19.99/mo**.

**Want Claude Code without a second vendor dance:** Claude Pro at **$20/mo** (or **$17/mo** annual).

**Want the broadest consumer AI toolkit and Codex/Work:** ChatGPT Plus at **$20/mo**.

If budget is tight and you mostly need "more than Free," test **ChatGPT Go ($8)** or **Google AI Plus ($4.99 US)** for a month, then upgrade to the Pro seat that matches your real bottleneck.
